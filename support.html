Frequently asked questions

How do I play with friends?

Tap HOST ROOM and share the 6-digit code; your friends tap JOIN and enter it. On the same Wi-Fi you can also join without a code. Up to 6 players (3v3), empty seats can be filled with bots.

I bought "Remove Ads" but I still see ads / I reinstalled the game.

Open the main menu, tap NO ADS, then RESTORE. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID / Google account you used for the purchase. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase and works on all your devices with that account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. On iOS request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

How can I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA/UK a PRIVACY button on the main menu reopens the consent form. On iOS you can also change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Achievements and leaderboards don't show up.

Sign in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android). The ACHIEVEMENTS and RANKINGS buttons appear on the STATS screen once you are signed in. Progress earned while signed out is sent the next time you sign in.

Can I delete my data?

The game has no accounts. Your nickname and stats are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the game. See the Privacy Policy for details.

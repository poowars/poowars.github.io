Privacy Policy

PooWars – by Zafo Games · Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the PooWars mobile game ("the Game") uses, why, and what choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to let you play with your friends.

1. Information we use

  • Nickname. The name you type when you start the Game. It is stored on your device and shown to the other players in the same match or room. Please do not use your real full name.
  • Anonymous player ID. When you play online, the Game signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services, which creates a random player ID. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any account.
  • Network information. To connect players in an online room, Unity Relay and Unity Lobby process your IP address and send real-time game data (positions, actions, scores) between players. This data is used only to run the match.
  • On-device settings. Sound settings, tutorial progress and similar preferences are saved only on your device.

We do not collect your email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment information. The Game has no chat.

2. Local (LAN) play

Local multiplayer connects devices on the same Wi-Fi network directly. No data is sent to our servers for local play.

3. Sharing room invites

If you tap "Invite", the Game opens your device's share sheet with a room code and link. You choose who to send it to; we do not see your messages or contacts.

4. Third-party services

Online play is provided by Unity Technologies (Unity Authentication, Lobby and Relay). Their handling of data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

The current version of the Game does not show advertisements and does not use analytics or tracking. If we add advertising or analytics in a future update, we will update this policy and the store privacy labels before that update is released.

5. Data retention and deletion

Online room data exists only while the room is open. The anonymous player ID is kept by Unity Gaming Services; uninstalling the Game removes it from your device and it can no longer be linked to you. To ask for deletion of any data associated with your player, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only information a player enters is a nickname. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Online traffic is encrypted by Unity Relay (DTLS). No system is 100% secure, but we use reputable providers and keep data to a minimum.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
